## 1. Core modules (core HR, attendance, shifts, leave, payroll, EOSB, loans, expenses, performance, recruitment, onboarding, documents, reports, ESS, plus extras: insurance/benefits, payments/cards, EWA)

### Takeaway
- **Bayzat** is the broadest suite. It covers HR, payroll, attendance and shifts, a benefits and insurance marketplace, a finance and payments layer, Bayzat Studio (workflows and no-code apps) and AI. It is the only one of the three with a real insurance and payments layer.
- **PalmHR** is an SME HR, payroll and time suite with tiered packaging, plus a services layer (Concierge, Outsourced HR).
- **Menaitech** is a mature, modular HCM family: MenaHR, MenaPAY, MenaTA, MenaME and MenaME-Plus+, Curio, MenaLMS and MenaAI-TA. It also has a cloud SME edition (MenaSME) and in-app bank loan offers (MenaLoan).
- None of the three has a verifiable earned-wage-access (EWA) product.

#### Bayzat
- Bayzat positions itself as one cloud platform for HR management, payroll, employee benefits and insurance — [Bayzat KSA platform](https://www.bayzat.com/ksa/platform); [Bayzat homepage](https://www.bayzat.com/).
- Buying is modular: a core package, plus add-on modules "like Benefits, Talent Management, or Finance as you grow" — [Bayzat KSA features](https://www.bayzat.com/ksa/features); [Bayzat small businesses](https://www.bayzat.com/small-businesses). A third-party listing describes tiered plans covering core HR, payroll, attendance, benefits administration and finance, with add-ons on top — [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/bayzat).
- **Payroll:** the system auto-populates the payroll variables (working days, leave deductions, expense additions, allowances), so HR "reviews and approves rather than builds from scratch" (vendor blog) — [Bayzat blog](https://blog.bayzat.com/payroll-software-bayzat/). KSA payroll auto-calculates employer and employee GOSI contributions and connects to Mudad for WPS — [Bayzat KSA payroll](https://www.bayzat.com/ksa/payroll).
- **EOSB:** calculates gratuity under Saudi Labour Law and generates the final settlement automatically — [Bayzat KSA payroll](https://www.bayzat.com/ksa/payroll).   - Sources: [Bayzat attendance blog](https://www.bayzat.com/blog/attendance-management-software-for-uae-gcc-teams/); [Bayzat shift scheduler](https://bayzat.com/shift-scheduler-and-attendance); [Bayzat KSA attendance](https://www.bayzat.com/ksa/attendance).

The "on-demand pay" page is a generic glossary entry, not a product — [Bayzat financial services](https://www.bayzat.com/financial-services); [Bayzat glossary: on-demand pay](https://www.bayzat.com/hr-glossary/on-demand-pay).

#### PalmHR (palm.hr)
- **Product lines:** People (core HR), Time (attendance), Pay (payroll), Project Time, Recruit (ATS) and Platform. Palm Hire is a separate recruiting product. Services are Outsourced HR and Concierge.
  - Sources: [People](https://palmhr.net/en/products/people); [Time](https://palmhr.net/en/products/time); [Pay](https://palmhr.net/en/products/pay); [Project Time](https://palmhr.net/en/products/project-time); [Recruit](https://palmhr.net/en/recruit); [Platform](https://palmhr.net/en/products/platform); [Palm Hire pricing](https://hiring.palmhr.net/pricing); [Outsourced HR](https://palmhr.net/en/outsourced-hr); [Concierge](https://palmhr.net/en/concierge/).
- **Tier contents** (source: [palmHR pricing](https://palmhr.net/en/pricing)):
  - **Starter** (up to 15 employees): no payroll and no advanced reporting.
  - **Core:** adds WPS-ready payroll processing, end-of-service benefit calculations, and multiple leave policies and approval flows.
  - **Professional:** adds multi-company and multi-currency, custom permissions, custom fields, white-labeling, accounting integrations and a public API.
  - **Enterprise:** adds custom development hours.
- Expense and loan management are automated. Payroll runs across "multiple countries, companies and currencies" — [palmHR homepage](https://palmhr.net/en).
- Modules span onboarding, time management, performance, payroll and compliance (per the October 2025 Wafeq partnership announcement) — [palm.hr x Wafeq](https://palmhr.net/en/resources/palmhr-and-wafeq-join-forces).
- The iOS listing names absence management, time attendance, mobile check-in/out, custom work schedules, remote work, compensation management, automated WPS payroll and GOSI compliance — [App Store: PalmHR](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1517422977).
- Policies auto-apply by location, contract type or department — [palmHR Time](https://palmhr.net/en/products/time).
- Biometric device integration (ZKTeco, CAMS) is an add-on — [palmHR integrations](https://palmhr.net/en/integrations); [palmHR pricing](https://palmhr.net/en/pricing).
- Accounting connectors on the Professional plan: Xero, QuickBooks, Zoho, Dynamics 365, NetSuite, Wafeq, Qoyod and Odoo — [palmHR integrations](https://palmhr.net/en/integrations).

- No expenses module, insurance marketplace, cards or EWA product was found.

### Inferences
- Bayzat's defensible extras against Shuoon are benefits and insurance (GCC broker heritage) plus payments. PalmHR differentiates on public SME pricing plus done-for-you services. Menaitech differentiates on enterprise breadth: talent, LMS, succession and multi-country payroll.
- All three cover the frontline basics Shuoon needs: attendance, shifts, leave, payroll and EOSB. Differentiation therefore has to come from Saudi integration depth, multi-branch operations UX, pricing and hosting rather than module count.
- An EWA or salary-advance product is an open gap across all three in KSA.

### Gaps
- Bayzat's recruitment and performance module details, and whether its insurance marketplace (UAE heritage) runs at full depth in KSA (CCHI claim unverified).
- PalmHR's documents module, performance depth, and Arabic UI. An Arabic website section exists ([palmhr.net/ar](https://palmhr.net/ar/resources/an-overview-of-saudi-arabia%E2%80%99s-online-workforce-management-platforms/)), but in-app Arabic was not confirmed.
- Menaitech's expenses and document management, and whether the Hijri calendar is supported. No source found for either.

## 2. Saudi presence and government integrations (GOSI, Mudad/WPS, Qiwa, Muqeem, ZATCA, Nitaqat, banks)

### Takeaway
- **What all three claim:** GOSI support and Mudad/WPS support.
- **Muqeem:** claimed by Bayzat and Menaitech. PalmHR claimed it in 2023, and its Concierge now covers it manually.
- **Qiwa:** only Bayzat makes a site claim (contracts and Saudization sync). PalmHR's Qiwa coverage appears to be content or service-based. Menaitech doesn't mention Qiwa.
- **No public evidence** says whether any of these are true API integrations or file and portal workflows.
- **Strongest Saudi heritage: Menaitech.** It has a Mudad MoU, Muqeem and Masdr links, a Saudization dashboard, and claims a Riyadh HQ.
- **Most aggressive KSA marketing: Bayzat.** It runs a KSA microsite and Arabic content, but it is UAE-headquartered.
- **PalmHR** is Riyadh-founded, but its recent web content leans toward UAE WPS banks.

### Inferences
- The highest-leverage combination for Shuoon's multi-branch frontline segment combines four patterns:
  - Bayzat-style kiosk plus face liveness and selfie with reason.
  - PalmHR-style offline mode plus rule-based policies by branch.
  - Menaitech-style Saudization dashboard and in-product Mudad and Muqeem actions.
  - A payroll run that is pre-populated, so HR only reviews exceptions.
- PalmHR's Concierge shows that SMEs will pay for done-for-you government transactions. Shuoon could productise this as an optional service tier.

### Gaps
- Hands-on UX evidence (screens, flows, click counts) could not be gathered. Neither product trials nor screenshots were accessible in this session.

## 8. Security and compliance (ISO 27001, SOC 2, PDPL, data hosting/in-Kingdom, SSO)
