# Competitor Profiles: Jisr (jisr.net) and ZenHR (zenhr.com) vs. Shuoon (Saudi multi-branch HR & payroll SaaS)

> Research date: 2026-10-30. Method note: direct fetches of jisr.net and zenhr.com were blocked by this session's network allowlist (the proxy returned 403 for both domains). The findings below come from search-engine extracts of vendor pages, vendor help centers (jisr.zendesk.com, zenhrsolutions.freshdesk.com), app-store listings, review sites and news coverage. Vendor-page claims are flagged as vendor claims. Where search metadata gave a page's age, I give an approximate date. Items older than 2024, or that could not be verified, are marked **(uncertain)** or **(pre-2024)**.

---

## Q1. Core modules: core HR, attendance (geofencing/biometrics), shifts, leave, payroll, EOSB, loans/advances, expenses, performance, ATS, onboarding, training, documents, org chart, reports, ESS

### Takeaway
Both products are full-suite Saudi HCM platforms covering core HR, attendance with geofencing and biometric devices, shifts, leave, KSA payroll with EOSB, performance, recruitment, onboarding and ESS. Jisr has pushed beyond HR into "HR, Talent & Spend": a Talent suite (2024), a Spend suite (2025) and "Jisr 2.0" HR+Finance (c. Sept 2025), adding expense claims, travel booking, earned-wage access through Qsalary, and salary benchmarking. Training comes through a partner, Lumofy. ZenHR is a modular HRMS sold as Value and Advanced bundles. It is strong on shift types, loans inside payroll, branded e-signed letters and multi-country (11 MENA) payroll. Its ATS (ZenATS) is an add-on, and I found no training/LMS module.

### Cited Findings

- The homepage positions Jisr as "All-in-one HR, Talent & Spend for modern KSA businesses" — [Jisr homepage](https://www.jisr.net/en)
- The About page lists a Talent suite launch in 2024 and a Spend suite launch in 2025 — [Jisr About](https://www.jisr.net/en/about)

It also lists corporate cards with custom spend limits. Named ecosystem partners are Neoleap, Tawuniya, Lumofy, Almosafer, Elite, MyFatoorah and Qsalary — [Jisr blog: Introducing Jisr 2.0](https://www.jisr.net/en/blog/introducing-jisr-2)

**Jisr — payroll, EOSB, loans/advances**

**Jisr — expense claims and spend**

**Jisr — talent: ATS, onboarding, performance, training, org chart**

## Q2. Saudi government integrations (GOSI, Mudad/WPS, Qiwa, Muqeem, Absher/Tamm, ZATCA, Nitaqat) and banks: true API vs. file export

### Takeaway
Both vendors run in-app, transactional ("native") integrations with **GOSI, Mudad/WPS and Muqeem**. **Qiwa** is the weak spot for both. Jisr's own pages contradict each other ("direct integration" on one page, "coming soon" on another). ZenHR claims Qiwa in a blog post and in 2026 funding coverage, but its official marketplace pages show GOSI, Mudad and Muqeem only. Both vendors still have file-based steps: Jisr reconciles GOSI through an uploaded wage file and has a manual encrypted WPS-file path for SAB-Awwal accounts. I found no evidence of ZATCA, Absher or Tamm integrations for either vendor. Both offer free Nitaqat calculators. ZenHR's reporting includes a Nitaqat dashboard, and Jisr's Saudization tracking inside the product is unconfirmed. Since 15 Apr 2026, Qiwa contract authentication determines Nitaqat counting, which makes Qiwa sync the most valuable gap for Shuoon to close.

### Cited Findings

**Jisr**
- Jisr claims "direct integration with all Saudi government systems": Mudad (WPS), GOSI, Muqeem and Qiwa. It also says it connects with ERP, banks and accounting programs — [Jisr: Integration with Government Systems](https://www.jisr.net/why-jisr/integration-with-government-systems-and-platforms)
- **Contradiction:** the Jisr features page describes Qiwa as something Jisr "will integrate soon" — [Jisr Features](https://www.jisr.net/en/features). A 2026 comparison says Jisr's own compliance page lists Qiwa as "coming soon" rather than live — [Zimyo: Best Payroll Software in Saudi Arabia (2026)](https://www.zimyo.me/insights/top-payroll-software-in-saudi-arabia/). The Talent suite lists a "Qiwa compliance" module without detail — [Jisr Talent Suite](https://www.jisr.net/en/talent-management)
- **GOSI (transactional):** from inside Jisr, users can update wages, change employee data, process terminations, and add Saudi and non-Saudi employees, at no additional fee — [Jisr product update: GOSI integration](https://www.jisr.net/en/product-updates/integration-with-gosi)
- A salary change can be synced to GOSI either while editing the salary or after saving it. Future-dated salary changes cannot be synced — [Jisr Help: Update employee salary in GOSI](https://jisr.zendesk.com/hc/en-sa/articles/13786989457297-Update-employee-salary-in-GOSI) (c. Aug 2025)
- Registering a Saudi employee in GOSI has prerequisites: the establishment's commercial registration must be integrated with Social Insurance, the employee needs a verified bank payment method, and the employee must be linked to the registering establishment — [Jisr Help: Register a Saudi employee to Social Insurance](https://jisr.zendesk.com/hc/en-sa/articles/21214102026513-Register-Add-a-Saudi-employee-to-Social-Insurance)
- **GOSI (file-based reconciliation):** the customer uploads the monthly GOSI wages file, and Jisr compares each employee's total salary against it and flags mismatches during payroll — [Jisr Help: Why upload the GOSI file](https://jisr.zendesk.com/hc/en-sa/articles/13322650150545-Stay-informed-and-compliant-with-Jisr-Why-do-I-need-to-upload-Gosi-file). GOSI and payment details can also be bulk-edited through an Excel import — [Jisr Help: Update payment method & GOSI details](https://jisr.zendesk.com/hc/en-sa/articles/13757377728401-How-to-modify-or-update-employees-Payment-Method-GOSI-Details)
- **Mudad/WPS (transactional, for supported banks):** setup is Settings → Online payment → Payment Method → "Register to Mudad", followed by authorizing the bank account and the employees allowed to process payroll payments. After payroll is confirmed, WPS files are uploaded automatically and payment processing can be tracked. Payment through Jisr and Mudad works only with certain banks — [Jisr Help: Activating Mudad integration](https://jisr.zendesk.com/hc/en-sa/articles/13753148309265-Activating-Mudad-integration-with-Jisr)
- **Mudad (manual file path):** for SAB-Awwal accounts, an authorized user downloads an encrypted WPS file from Jisr's payroll payment tracking and uploads it, unopened, to the Mudad Compliance System — [Jisr Help: WPS file for SAB-Awwal](https://jisr.zendesk.com/hc/en-sa/articles/13760202826385-How-to-download-WPS-file-for-SAB-Awwal-account)
- The vendor claims multi-bank integration, compliance checks and "zero WPS penalties across 5000+ companies" (unverified; the company count is inconsistent with the 4,700+ customers in its Jan 2026 press release) — [Jisr homepage](https://www.jisr.net/en)
- A 2026 third-party review lists "WPS Auto Upload" as free for the first 6 months, which implies a paid add-on afterwards **(uncertain)** — [hr.software](https://www.hr.software/reviews/jisr)
- **Muqeem (transactional):** iqama renewal; issuing, extending, cancelling and reprinting exit/re-entry visas; final exit; passport-validity extension; and Muqeem reports, all from inside Jisr — [Jisr product update: Muqeem](https://www.jisr.net/en/product-updates/integration-with-muqeem); [Jisr updates: "fully integrated with Moqeem"](https://updates.jisr.net/en/jisr-is-fully-integrated-with-moqeem-now)
- **Nitaqat:** a free calculator estimates the Nitaqat level and how many Saudis must be hired — [Jisr Nitaqat calculator](https://www.jisr.net/en/hr-tools/nitaqat-calculator). Jisr's ATS guide describes recruitment software that integrates with Qiwa to calculate the Saudization rate and show it against the Nitaqat target. This is an educational guide, not a confirmed product spec **(uncertain)** — [Jisr ATS guide](https://www.jisr.net/en/blog/guide-to-applicant-tracking-system). It requires Odoo Enterprise with the Accounting and Employees modules — [Naqlah](https://www.naqlah.com.sa/integrations/jisr). **ZenHR**
